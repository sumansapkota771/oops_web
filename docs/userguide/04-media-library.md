# 4. Media library

← [Back to the guide index](README.md)

**Site → Media library** holds every photo and video used on the website. Whenever a form asks you to choose a photo or video, you choose it from here.

## Browsing

At the top of the library:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Search files…** | Finds files by name |
| **Folder** | Narrow to one folder (for example `gallery/general` or `uploads`) |
| **Photos & videos / Photos / Videos** | Filter by type |
| **N files** | How many match |

Files appear as square thumbnails, 60 at a time — press **Load more** for the rest. Videos have a small camera icon. Click a thumbnail to open its details.

## Uploading

You can upload in two places:

- **Media library** — press **Upload** (or drag files anywhere onto the page).
- **Any photo/video picker** — when you press *Choose photo* in an edit form, the pop-up has an **Upload** button too. After a single-photo upload the new photo is selected for you.

You can upload many files at once. A blue bar shows progress ("Uploading 2 of 5…").

New files go into the **folder currently selected** in the library; if none is selected they go into a folder called `uploads`. Pick the right folder first to keep things tidy.

### What can you upload?

| Type | Accepted | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Photos** | JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF… | Resized automatically in your browser into three sizes (480, 960 and 1600 px wide) and converted to WebP, plus a tiny blurred preview. The site picks the right size for each visitor's screen, so pages load fast. |
| **Videos** | MP4 or WebM, **under 50 MB** | Uploaded as is. A poster (cover) picture is made automatically from the first seconds. |
| **Logos / graphics** | SVG | Uploaded as is. |
| **Documents** | PDF | Uploaded as is. |

Not supported: camera RAW files (such as `.CR2`) — export a JPG first. Videos larger than 50 MB are refused.

**For a long film** (like the full OOPS! movie): upload it to YouTube and paste the link in *Pages → Homepage → Video section → YouTube link*.

**Tips for good photos**

- Upload the original JPG — do not shrink it yourself; the library does it.
- A photo wider than about 1,600 px is more than enough. Very small photos (under 800 px wide) can look blurry on big screens.
- Landscape photos suit hero banners; portrait photos suit offer posters.
- Keep video clips short (10–30 s) and compressed (720p) for the hero background.

## File details

Click any thumbnail to open its details:

- **Preview** of the photo, or a player for videos.
- **Size** in pixels, **file size** in MB, and the **folder**.
- **Path (used by the site)** — the file's internal name. The copy button copies it; you rarely need it.
- **Description (alt text)** — type a short description, e.g. *Children climbing the rope course*. Press **Save description**. It is read aloud to blind visitors and helps Google Images. It is filled in from the file name at first, so it's worth improving for important photos.
- **Delete** — removes the file **permanently**.

> **Before you delete a file:** any page that uses it will show an empty gap until you pick another picture. If unsure, leave it. Deleting a photo does *not* remove it from lists that already use it — you must pick a replacement there.

## Replacing a photo

There is no "replace" button, because each upload gets its own name. To swap a photo:

1. Upload the new photo.
2. Open the item or page that uses the old one, press **Change** on the photo field, and choose the new one.
3. Save.
4. Optionally delete the old file from the library.

## Where photos are used

| Field type | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| **Single photo** (cover photo, poster, logo) | Press **Choose photo** to pick, **Change** to swap, **Remove** to clear. |
| **Several photos** (gallery, collage) | Press **Add photos**, tick several in the picker, then **Add N selected**. Hover a photo to reveal ◀ ▶ (reorder) and 🗑 (remove from this list — the file stays in the library). |
| **Video** | **Choose video** — only videos are shown. |
