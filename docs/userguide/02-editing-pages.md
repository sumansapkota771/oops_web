# 2. Editing pages

← [Back to the guide index](README.md)

Use **Pages** in the left menu to edit the words, buttons, photos and videos on each page of the website.
The list in **Pages** shows each page with its public address.

| Page | Public address |
| --- | --- |
| Homepage | `/` |
| About | `/about` |
| Attractions | `/attractions` |
| Prices & Offers | `/prices-offers` |
| Events | `/events` |
| Gallery | `/gallery` |
| Contact | `/contact` |
| FAQ | `/faq` |
| Rules & Terms | `/terms` |

> **Pages vs Content.** *Pages* hold the headings and fixed text of a page. *Content* holds the lists that appear on pages (the attractions, the offers, the FAQ questions…). For example: the heading above the offers is edited in **Pages → Prices & Offers**; the offers themselves are edited in **Content → Offers**.

## How a page editor is laid out

Every page editor has two parts:

### 1. Search-engine box (top of every page)

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Page name (admin only)** | A label for you. Customers never see it. |
| **Browser tab / Google title** | The title shown in the browser tab and as the blue link in Google results. Keep it under about 60 characters. |
| **Google description** | The grey sentence under the title in Google results. Aim for 120–160 characters. |
| **Share image (optional)** | The picture shown when the page is shared on Facebook or WhatsApp. If empty, the default from [Site settings](05-site-settings.md#search-engines-seo) is used. |

### 2. "Page content" — one box per section

Most sections follow the same pattern:

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Eyebrow** | The small tag above the heading, e.g. `OUR STORY`. |
| **Title** | The main heading. Required. Use `*asterisks*` to colour words. Headings display in **BOLD UPPERCASE** automatically — you do not need to type capitals. |
| **Subtitle** | A short paragraph under the heading (not on every section). |

Some sections add extras: a **button** (Button text + Link), a **photo**, a **video**, or a **list** of points.

Press **Save changes** when you are done, then **View on site ↗** to check.

## What you can edit on each page

### Homepage

| Section | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **Hero** | Headline, subtitle, main button, second button, **background video**, video poster image, small badges (e.g. "Open daily 11 AM – 8 PM") |
| **Numbers section** | Heading only. The numbers themselves live in [Site settings → Big numbers](05-site-settings.md#big-numbers-stats) |
| **Attractions section** | Heading, subtitle, button |
| **Offers section** | Heading, subtitle, button |
| **Video section** | Heading, video, poster image, or a **YouTube link** (used *instead of* the uploaded video — the best choice for long films) |
| **Events section** | Heading, subtitle, button, photo, bullet points |
| **Photo marquee** | Heading and the scrolling photos |
| **Reviews section** | Heading, optional **Google rating** (e.g. `4.6`), link to your Google reviews. The reviews themselves are in **Content → Reviews** |
| **Hall of Fame section** | Heading, subtitle, button. Photos come from the Gallery |
| **Partner brands section** | Heading and subtitle. Logos come from the Gallery |
| **Visit us section** | Heading and subtitle. Hours, address, map and phones come from Site settings |

### About

Hero (with background photo) · **About us** (title + paragraphs) · **Vision** · **Mission** · **Who we serve** (list of groups) · **Facilities** section heading · **Photo collage** (4 photos).

### Attractions

Hero · **Good to know** (heading + list of safety points, e.g. grip socks, age and height checks).
The attractions themselves are in **Content → Attractions**.

### Prices & Offers

| Section | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **Hero** | Heading and subtitle |
| **Powerplay section** | Heading, subtitle, **Powerplay poster**, the four **table headings** (columns 1–4), and the **Terms & conditions** list. The rows of the table are in **Content → Powerplay table** |
| **Quick facts** | Label/value pairs such as *Minimum first recharge — Rs 1,000* |
| **All-day deals section** | Heading and subtitle for the weekly deals |
| **Other offers section** | Heading, subtitle, and the text shown when there are no offers right now |
| **Khalti badge** | Switch the badge on/off and edit its text. It shows on offers marked "Also available on Khalti" |

### Events

Hero (with background photo) · **Upcoming events** section (including the "no events" text) · **Banquet hall** (photo and points) · **Book now** (phone, email and the **thank-you message** customers see after sending the form).

### Gallery, Contact, FAQ

- **Gallery** — hero only. Photos are in **Content → Gallery photos**.
- **Contact** — hero, and the **Form** section (heading and thank-you message).
- **FAQ** — hero and the **Bottom box** (title and text of the call-to-action under the questions). Questions are in **Content → FAQ**.

### Rules & Terms

Hero · **Introduction** · **Sections** (a list of groups; each has a **Key** — `rules`, `refund` or `waiver` — and a **Title**). The points inside each group are edited in **Content → Rules & policies**.
Do not change a section's Key unless the developer asks you to, or the group will disappear from the page.

## Tips

- Keep headings short. They display in capitals and take more room.
- Use the **Subtitle** for the detail, not the heading.
- Check your page on your phone as well as a computer.
- If a photo looks wrong (cropped, dark), pick a different one — the site crops photos to fit their space. Landscape photos are safest for hero backgrounds.
