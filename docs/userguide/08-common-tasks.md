# 8. Common tasks

← [Back to the guide index](README.md)

Step-by-step recipes for the jobs you will do most often. After each one, press **View website ↗** (Dashboard) to check the result.

## Add a limited-time offer (e.g. a festival deal)

1. **Media library →** upload the offer poster (portrait works best).
2. **Content → Offers → + New offer**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**, **Tagline**, **Schedule** (e.g. `Dashain week · 11 AM – 8 PM`)
   - **Type** → *Other offer (limited time)*
   - **Price** and **Price note**, **What's included**, **Notes / fine print**
   - **Poster** → *Choose photo*
   - **Ends on** — so it switches off by itself (**Starts on** only displays the start date; it does not hide the offer until then, so save the offer when you want it to appear)
4. **Save changes**.
5. Optional: promote it with the [announcement bar](#show-an-announcement-bar) (link it to `/prices-offers#other-offers`).

The offer appears under **Other offers** on Prices & Offers, and vanishes after the end date.

## Change a weekly deal (e.g. Unlimited Friday or 30 Taps Thrill)

1. **Content → Offers**, click the offer.
2. Edit the **Schedule**, **Price**, **What's included** or the **Poster**.
3. **Save changes**.

## Update the Powerplay recharge table

1. **Content → Powerplay table**.
2. Click a row to change its numbers, or **+ New Powerplay row** to add one. Use ▲▼ to put rows in order (smallest recharge first).
3. Switch on **Highlight this row** for the best-value row.
4. Terms and conditions: **Pages → Prices & Offers → Powerplay section → Terms & conditions**.

## Add a new game or ride

1. Upload photos (or choose existing ones later in the form).
2. **Content → Attractions → + New attraction**.
3. Enter the **Name**, pick a **Category**, add a **Tagline** and **Description**.
4. Choose a **Cover photo** (required) and add more under **Photo gallery**.
5. Add **Good-to-know notes** (age, height, grip socks), and tick **Grip socks required** if it applies.
6. Switch on **Feature on the homepage** if you want it on the front page.
7. **Save changes**. Its page is `/attractions/<page-address>`.

To **retire** a game temporarily, open **Content → Attractions** and click the eye icon on its row.

## Add an upcoming event

1. **Content → Upcoming events → + New upcoming event**.
2. Enter **Title**, **Date**, **Time**, **Description**, a **Poster**, and optionally a **Button** (e.g. *Book now* → `/events`).
3. **Save changes**. After the date passes, it hides itself.

## Add photos to the Gallery

1. **Content → Gallery photos → + New gallery photo**.
2. Choose the **Album**, press **Choose photo** (upload one if needed) and, for *Hall of Fame*, type the person's name as the **Caption**.
3. **Save changes**. Repeat for each photo.

New photos go to the end of the album; use ▲▼ or **⤒** in the list to reorder.

## Add a Hall of Fame winner or partner logo

Same as above, but choose the **Hall of Fame** or **Partner brands** album. Hall of Fame photos need a **Caption** (the name) to appear on the homepage. For partner logos, a PNG or SVG with a clean background works best.

## Add a Google review

1. Copy the review text from Google Maps.
2. **Content → Reviews → + New review**: add **Name**, **Stars**, **Review**, **When**, and optional **Badge**.
3. **Save changes**. Update the overall rating under *Pages → Homepage → Reviews section*.

## Show an announcement bar

1. **Site settings → Announcement bar**.
2. Switch on **Show the announcement bar**.
3. Type the **Text**, and a **Link** if you want it clickable.
4. Optionally set **Hide automatically after**.
5. **Save changes** — it appears at the top of every page.

## Change opening hours (e.g. for a festival)

1. **Site settings → Opening hours**.
2. Update **Hours** and **Note**, plus **Opens** and **Closes** in 24-hour format (e.g. `10:00`, `21:00`).
3. **Save changes**. Remember to change them back afterwards — or use the announcement bar for one-day changes.

## Change a phone number or email

1. **Site settings → Contact**, edit, **Save changes**. It updates the header, footer, Contact page and homepage.
2. The Events page has its own phone and email: **Pages → Events → Book now**.

## Update an FAQ answer

**Content → FAQ**, click the question, edit, **Save changes**. Use the search box to find it.

## Change the homepage video or headline

**Pages → Homepage → Hero** — edit the headline and subtitle, and use **Background video** to choose a new clip (upload an MP4 under 50 MB first). For the longer movie section, paste a YouTube link in **Video section**.

## Change the "big numbers"

**Site settings → Big numbers**. Edit the number, the suffix (`+`) and the label. Save.

## Respond to a Book Now enquiry

1. **Enquiries** — filter by **New**.
2. Call or message the customer using the phone/email on the card.
3. Change the status to **Contacted**, and to **Closed** once it's finished.

## Hand over access to a new staff member

1. **Users → Add a user** — name, email, role **Editor**, leave the password empty.
2. Copy the generated password and send it privately.
3. Ask them to sign in at `/admin` and choose their own password.

## A good routine

| When | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Check **Enquiries** for new messages |
| Weekly | Check offers and events are current; check the announcement bar |
| Monthly | **Backup all content**; review users (disable leavers) |
| Before a festival | Prepare offers with an **Ends on** date, then update hours and the announcement bar a few days ahead |
